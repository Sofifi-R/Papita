Pet Nova

The app that helps you care for your pet's health and safety. Keep their medical history, receive vaccine reminders, activate Paw Alert in emergencies, and find them on the map, all from your mobile phone.

We believe every pet deserves the best start. At Pet Nova, we develop tools to help you care for their health, well-being, and happiness, accompanying you during every stage of their lives.

Everything for your pet's well-being

Everything your favorite pet needs, on a single platform.

Alerta Paw

Si tu mascota se pierde, activa la alerta y avisa en segundos a la comunidad y a las veterinarias cercanas, con su foto y su última ubicación.

Mapa Pet

Encuentra veterinarias, servicios y lugares pet-friendly cerca de ti, y consulta la última ubicación registrada de tu mascota.

Historial médico

Guarda vacunas, citas, tratamientos y alergias en un solo lugar, listos para mostrarlos al veterinario cuando los necesites.

Calendario inteligente

Recibe recordatorios de vacunas, desparasitaciones, baños y paseos para que nunca se te pase una fecha importante.

Comunidad

Conecta con otros dueños de mascotas, comparte consejos y ayuda a encontrar mascotas perdidas en tu zona.

Connect with local services near you with Pet Map

With Pet Map you find veterinarians, services and pet-friendly spots near you, and see the last recorded location of your beloved pets in just a few seconds.
