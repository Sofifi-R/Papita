Privacy Policy – The Basics

A privacy policy discloses the ways a website or app collects, uses, shares and protects the data of its visitors and users. At Pet Nova, we are committed to protecting your privacy and that of your pets' information.

Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and password when you sign up.
  • Pet data: name, species, breed, photos, vaccines, allergies, medications and medical history logs that you choose to enter.
  • Calendar data: the appointments and reminders you create (bath day, vaccines, vet visits).
  • Messages: the details you send us through the contact form.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information that helps us keep the service working.

How we use your information

  • To provide the features of the app: medical profile, Smart Care Calendar, Pet Map and Paw Alert.
  • To send reminders and important service notices.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To improve and secure Pet Nova.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers that help us run the app, when the law requires it, or when you decide to share something yourself, such as publishing a Paw Alert.

Location & Paw Alert

Pet Map uses your location to show nearby veterinarians and pet-friendly places. When you activate Paw Alert, the photo and last known location of your pet are shared with the community and nearby clinics. You can turn off location access in your device settings at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to close your account. Send your request to the email below and we will answer as soon as possible.

Minors

Pet Nova is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a minor has given us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we encourage you to use a strong password and keep it private.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Write to petnovitas2027@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
